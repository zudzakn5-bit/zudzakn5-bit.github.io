Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

zudzakn5 Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by a single individual to upload videos to YouTube channels that the same individual owns or manages.

What the app accesses

The app requests only the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which is used solely to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and privacy/schedule settings on the owner's own channels.

What the app collects

The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data or Google user data to anyone. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only by the owner.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
